# Acknowledging the National Supercomputing Mission in Publications

Any research conducted using the computational resources of the PARAM Rudra 20 PF HPC System must appropriately acknowledge the facility in all resulting research outputs, including journal articles, research papers, conference papers, posters, presentations, reports, and other scholarly publications or materials.

Publishing research outputs resulting from the use of the facility may also strengthen your research profile and may be considered as an additional contribution during future shortlisting, evaluation, or selection processes, where applicable.

“This research used computational resources at  PARAM Rudra 20 PF HPC System provided by the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC) under the National Supercomputing Mission (NSM), India’s flagship initiative jointly steered by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and the Department of Science and Technology (DST).”
